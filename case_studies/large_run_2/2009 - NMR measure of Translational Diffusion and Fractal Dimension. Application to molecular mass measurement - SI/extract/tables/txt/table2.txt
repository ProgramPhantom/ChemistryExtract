## B. Molecular mass determination

## B.1. Data analysis

The data were analyzed with a home written scipy/python program freely available upon from the authors request. In the cases of oligosaccharides and denatured peptides, the published values were used. d F and C r values were obtained from a direct weighted fit of equation 6 on the data, which did not require to take the log of the analysed values. Complete error analysis was performed as detailed in the next section. When no experimental errors were available, it was assumed that the diffusion coefficients were measured with a 10% error bar. Results are shown in 2.

Obtained R coefficients ranged from 0.995 to 0.99999 for polymer studies, R was 0.976 for proteins, 0.977 for small molecules in water, 0.983 for small molecules in CDCl 3 , 0.992 for linear alcanes, 0.994 for denatured peptides, and 0.9994 for oligosaccharides. Uncertainties are given by a covariance matrix analysis.
