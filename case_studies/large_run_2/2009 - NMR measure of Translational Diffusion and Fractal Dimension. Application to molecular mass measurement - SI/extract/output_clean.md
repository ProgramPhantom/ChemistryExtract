## A. Experimental Details

## A.1. Protein study

Protein data were collected on a Bruker AMX-600 spectrometer with a field gradient accessory capable to deliver z-field gradients up to 50 Gcm -1 . In all NMR experiments, the probe temperature was maintained at 22.8 o C and 5mm sample tubes were used. The LED spin-echo pulse sequence with bipolar field gradients was used for all the data reported. Measurement of 1 H NMR spectra in aqueous solution required the suppression of the large resonance from H 2 O and we used a modified version of the LED sequence with a WATERGATE scheme included after the final reading pulse .

Sequence delays were ∆ = 200 ms (diffusion delay), τ = 1.2 ms (after gradient recovery delay), and T e = 3ms (LED recovery delay). For each data set, 2048 complex points were collected for each 35 experiments in which the gradient strength was exponentially incremented from 1 to 47.5 Gcm -1 . The gradient duration δ/ 2 was adjusted to observe a near complete signal loss at 47.5 Gcm -1 . Typically, the δ/ 2 delay was chosen in the 0.7 - 1.5 ms range. A 1s recycle delay was used between scans for all data shown. In all cases, the WATERGATE gradients were kept at 4 Gcm -1 for 1 msec, and the total acquisition time ranged from 30 minutes to 14 hours depending on the sample.

We purchased BPTI (Bovine Pancreatic Trypsin Inhibitor, Sigma), ubiquitin (Sigma), cytochrome C (Sigma), ribonuclease A (Pharmacia), lysozyme (Boehringer Mannheim), myoglobin (Sigma), β -lactoglobulin A (Sigma), chymotrypsinogen A (Pharmacia), and ovalbumin (Sigma) as lyophilized salt-free powders. The Glutathione S-Transferase (GST) was released from GST-fusion protein by thrombin cleavage. Charybdotoxin , cardiotoxinγ , and neurotoxin α were obtained from A. M´ enez. Thioredoxin was obtained from F. de Lamotte-Gu´ ery as presented in . Porine MOMP was obtained from G.Labesse as presented in .

All the proteins having a molecular weight higher than 8 kD were desalted with the following protocol. A quantity of 2 mg of protein was dissolved in 3 ml of 50 mM phosphate buffer (pH 5.8) and centrifuged at 10 o C, 4000 g in a 4 ml tube (membrane Biomax5, Ultrafree-4, Millipore). The sample was concentrated again to approximately 200 400 µ l. Three wash cycles were applied. Protein concentrations were determined by UV absorbance measurement at 280 nm wavelength (410 nm for myoglobin). All samples were prepared for a protein concentration of 3 mg/ml in a phosphate buffer (50 mM, pH5.8, in water (H 2 O:D 2 O, 90%:10%) containing 0.5 mM of Tris.

For each data set, the spectral axis was processed with sine-bell, and Fourier transform was applied in order to obtain 1024 real points. A spline baseline correction was finally applied. The columns (axis of varying gradient) of this data-set were then processed for inverse Laplace transform using the Inverse Laplace technique using the Gifa software

. The DOSY reconstruction was realized with 490 points in the diffusion dimension and 2000 MaxEnt iterations. For each protein, the diffusion coefficient was calculated as the mean value of the diffusion coefficients observed for the 15 most intense columns in the 0.22 - 2.46 ppm region. The uncertainty on the measure was estimated from the standard deviation σ on these values, and chosen at ± σ (95% confidence level). ## A.2. Polymer study

Polymers were measured either on a 600 MHz (PS in toluene) or on a 400 MHz spectrometer (other studies). Polyethyleneoxide (PEO) and polystyrene (PS) calibrated monodisperse polymers were purchased from Polymer Labs or Amerian Polymer Standards Corporation, the reported polydispersity index being typically between 1.02 and 1.10. Materials were used as received. PEO standards were stored in a refrigerator to minimize degradation. Measures were performed at 30 ◦ C. The diffusion time was typi- cally 50 ms, increased to a range of 100 to 350 ms for the analysis of ultra-high molecular mass polymers, such as the 7,800 and 18,000 kDa PS samples. The number of scan was adapted to the sample, but never less than eight scans. The total experiment time, including a 4 min temperature equilibration step, ranged from 15 minutes to several hours per sample All experiment (except for PS in toluene) were acquired as follows. The 1 H PFG NMR diffusion experiments utilized a Bruker Avance-400 spectrometer operating at a 1 H frequency of 400.1 MHz, equipped with a 5 mm multinuclear broadband-observe z-axis gradient probe capable of generating 55 G/cm field strengths. The PFG NMR experiments employed a convection-compensating double stimulated-echo pulse sequence with three 0.6-ms sinusoidal-shaped spoiler gradient pulses. Smoothed-square gradient pulses with durations typically in the range of 4-14 ms were used for each of the four diffusion pulses. eight spectra were obtained using varying gradient strengths.

## B. Molecular mass determination

## B.1. Data analysis

The data were analyzed with a home written scipy/python program freely available upon from the authors request. In the cases of oligosaccharides and denatured peptides, the published values were used. d F and C r values were obtained from a direct weighted fit of equation 6 on the data, which did not require to take the log of the analysed values. Complete error analysis was performed as detailed in the next section. When no experimental errors were available, it was assumed that the diffusion coefficients were measured with a 10% error bar. Uncertainties are given by a covariance matrix analysis.
