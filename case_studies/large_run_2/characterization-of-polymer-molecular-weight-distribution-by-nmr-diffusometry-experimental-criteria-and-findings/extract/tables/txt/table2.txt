Validation of eq 4 not only establishes the basis to determine C dilute * for polymer characterization but holds two important applications for polymer characterization by di ff usion. First, D polymer will be insensitive to C variation within a certain range if polymer chains adopt the globular structure, i.e., α = 1/3. Under this circumstance, polymer chains become highly folded that drastically diminish the impact of chain interactions on D polymer and the dependence of C dilute * on M will vanish. Many proteins are well known to form the globular structure, and we use albumin of egg white ( M ∼ 43k) to examine the above prediction. Figure 5 compares di ff usion distribution P ( D ) between egg albumin protein and monodisperse PEO standard ( M = 32.5k) in D2O. P ( D ) remains invariant with protein concentration, in contrast to the large variation of P ( D ) with PEO concentration, which veri fi es the prediction stated above. This further provides a new avenue to evaluate the folding of protein in solution, which is an important topic in bioscience.
