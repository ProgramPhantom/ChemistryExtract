3.2. Copolymer Composition with DOSY. To determine the molar mass distributions of block copolymers with DOSY, we want to adopt the same approach of the SEC-UV-RI dual detector analysis to the DOSY studies. Therefore, four PSb -PMMA copolymer solutions of different compositions and chain lengths were prepared for the copolymer analysis (Table 1). PSb -PMMA copolymers were chosen because of the possibility to determine diffusion coefficients and signal intensities for each copolymer block separately by analyzing separated signals. The aromatic protons were chosen for the PS block, and the methoxy group was used for the PMMA block.

Self-diffusion coefficients of copolymers were determined by varying the gradient strength in 128 linear steps between 3 and 98% of the maximum gradient strength. The gradient strength and diffusion delays were chosen such as that the signal intensity of the methoxy group at 3.6 ppm was &lt;1% for the strongest gradient (98%) compared to the signal intensity at 3% gradient strength. According to these preparation conditions, the 2D-DOSY experiments were performed. The results are illustrated in Figure 2 showing the PMMA methoxy group and the aromatic protons of PS.
