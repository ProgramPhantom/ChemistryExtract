The exponent value of 0.60 ± 0.01 is in very good agreement with the Zimm model (33), which predicts an exponent value of 0.6 for flexible polymers in a good solvent.

Note that in some cases the values of self-diffusion coefficients were also measured on more diluted solutions (0.4 mg/0.7 ml and 0.2 mg/0.7 ml), however, these values remain constant within the experimental error. This observation confirms that the solutions used for measuring the diffusion coefficient were sufficiently diluted.

It is noteworthy that for all PEG samples, the selfdiffusion coefficient of d2-TCE remains equal within the experimental error. This means that no retarding effects on the solvent diffusion are detectable, further confirming that the solutions we used were sufficiently diluted.
