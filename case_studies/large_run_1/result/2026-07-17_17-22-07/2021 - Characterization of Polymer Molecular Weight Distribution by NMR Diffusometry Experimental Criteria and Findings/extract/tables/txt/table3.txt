We have demonstrated the critical role of C dilute * to specify the su ffi ciently dilute condition for M and MWD characterization and further linked it to chain conformation, M and PDI. Polymer and solvent di ff usion respond di ff erently to concentration variation, highlighting the unique role of chain interactions even at concentrations signi fi cantly below C * . Quantitative analysis indicates that D polymer measurement above the su ffi ciently dilute condition would lead to a systematic and potentially large error in M and MWD analysis. These fi ndings help to understand the data disparity in the literature and provide useful guidance toward M /MWD characterization by NMR di ff usion measurements. The study o ff ers key insights that lead to the discovery of the invariant macromolecular di ff usion on concentration for globular structure and provides a di ff erent approach for characterizing polymer molecular weight and chain architecture.
