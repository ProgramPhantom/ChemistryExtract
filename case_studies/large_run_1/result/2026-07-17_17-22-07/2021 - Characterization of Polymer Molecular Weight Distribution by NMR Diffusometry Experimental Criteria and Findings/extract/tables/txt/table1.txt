For GPC analysis, the sample was prepared in a HLPC grade tetrahydrofuran at a concentration of 2 mg/mL and allowed to dissolve for 2 h on a fl atbed shaker. The standards were run on a Waters Model e2695 at a nominal fl ow rate of 1.0 mL/min of HPLC grade tetrahydrofuran. Two 5 μ m mixed-C columns from Polymer Laboratories were used and held at 35 ° C with an injection volume of 100 μ L. A Shodex RI-201 refractive index detector was used at a temperature of 35 ° C. Data was acquired with Polymer Laboratories Cirrus version 2.0, and 16 narrow polystyrene standards from Polymer Laboratories, fi tting to a third-order polynomial curve over the range of 2750 -0.580 kg/mol, were applied to calibrate the system.

For NMR analysis, PMMA and PS samples were dissolved in deuterated chloroform (CDCl3). PEO and egg white protein samples were dissolved in deuterated water (D 2 O). Typically, ∼ 10 -15 mg of sample was accurately weighed on a balance (accurate to 0.1 mg) to prepare a concentrated stock solution, e.g., 1 wt %. An aliquot of the stock solution was then transferred and weighed into a glass vial and further diluted to the target concentration by adding a speci fi c amount of solvent with 1 mM chromium(III) acetylacetonate (as the relaxation agent). The dilution level was further cross checked spectroscopically for consistency. For PMMA, 825 ± 5 mg of the above-diluted solution was consistently added into a 5 mm NMR tube. Similarly, 590 ± 10 mg of each PEO or protein sample solution was consistently added into a 5 mm NMR tube. All of the samples were freshly prepared and equilibrated at 25 ° C for 10 min prior to any di ff usion measurement. Solvent evaporation was negligible for most of the experiments (&lt;1 h) and the NMR tube was sealed when experimental time lasted longer than 5 h. 1 H NMR di ff usion measurements were conducted on a 400 MHz Bruker Avance spectrometer equipped with a broad band 5 mm probe. The probe temperature was calibrated and controlled at 25 ± 0.1 ° C. Gradient calibration was performed by measuring di ff usion of residual 1 H2O in D2O to yield a self-di ff usion coe ffi cient of 1.90 × 10 -9 m 2 /s. 26 Pulsed gradient double spin echo (PGDSE) 27 was employed to measure PMMA and PEO di ff usion successively by monitoring the variation of the I -OCH3 and I -OCH2 signal as a function of magnetic fi eld gradient g . Pulsed gradient double stimulated echo (PGDSTE) 28 was employed to measure PS and protein di ff usion successively. Around 4 -4800 scans were recorded to generate adequate spectra sensitivity with a total repetition time being 4 -5 × of the longitudinal relaxation time T 1 .
