However, PEF standards  for  calibration  are  not  available, and therefore, we deemed PET standards the appropriate choice due to its similar structure  and  size  distribution  characteristics. We found the reliability of this approach  confirmed  by  the  results  in Table  1:  PET  and  PEF  molecules  of  the same  (absolute)  molecular  weight  do show the same diffusion coefficient, e.g., D = 3.2 × 10 -11 m 2 s -2 (by  DOSY) for both PET and PEF of about 61 000 mol -1 (by MALS). Note that this same approach does not apply to SEC measurements of PEF samples. In fact, initially applied SEC analysis  of  our  PEF  samples  using  PET standards  resulted  in  an  overprediction of molecular weight by roughly 2-2.5 fold, compared with SEC analysis evaluated with MALS. Similar overprediction was found using polymethylmethacrylate and polystyrene (PS)  standards.  An  explanation  for  the  observed  discrepancy - exceeding the scope of this work - is ongoing.
