## Conclusions

It has been shown, in the controlled polymerization of L-lactide, that using a combination of di ff erent NMR techniques (standard and DOSY) allows studying on-line the kinetics of polymerization together with the determination of M n, M w and Đ .

Moreover, in the same experiment carried out in a single NMR tube, it was possible to analyze the structure of poly(L-lactide).
