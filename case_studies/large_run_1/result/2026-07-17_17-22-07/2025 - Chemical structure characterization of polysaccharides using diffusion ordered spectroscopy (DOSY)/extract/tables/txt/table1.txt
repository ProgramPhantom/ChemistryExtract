## 3.1. Carbohydrate content and monosaccharide composition of the β -glucans samples

The carbohydrate content, as well as the monosaccharide composition of LMN and YBGS were determined, and the results are presented in Table 1.

Data from PEA2 -SP , a β -glucan fraction isolated from Pleurotus eryngii , (de Carvalho et al., 2024) were included in Table 1 for comparison. The carbohydrate content of all samples was &gt; 90 %. The monosaccharide composition showed that glucose was the major component in all fractions, ranging from 87 to 99 mol%.

## 3.2. Mw distribution of the β -glucans samples

The M w, Đ and recovery from SEC column obtained for LMN and YBGS are presented in Table 2 and the elution profile in Fig. S1. LMN showed a homogeneous profile with M w of 5 kDa ( Đ 1.002). Differently, YBGS showed three different peaks, with Đ values of each peak varying from 1.1 to 1.5, indicating that this fraction was highly heterogeneous regarding the M w distribution. The main peak was constituted of a pool of polysaccharides with an estimated M w of 8 kDa. In addition, populations of polysaccharides of M w 35 and 240 kDa were also identified in YBGS .

The M w and recovery from SEC column for PEA2 -SP was previously determined and were included in Table 2 for comparison. Briefly, PEA2 -SP presents an apparent M w of 36 kDa. Populations with M w higher than 2120 kDa were also identified. The higher M w populations present both in YBGS and PEA2 -SP are attributed to the presence of aggregates in aqueous solutions.
