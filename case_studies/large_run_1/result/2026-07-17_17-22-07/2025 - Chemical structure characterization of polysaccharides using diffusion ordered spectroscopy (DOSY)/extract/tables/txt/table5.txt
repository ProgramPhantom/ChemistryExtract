For YBGS , DOSY showed that units F (6-linked), A (3-linked) and G (3-linked attached to the O-6 of the adjacent unit) presented similar diffusion time. Units A and G diffusing at the same time is in accordance with the presence of a 3-linked β -glucan branched at O -6 by a 3-linked glucose side chain, as described in literature (Fleet &amp; Manners, 1976;

Manners et al., 1973) (Fig. 7). From the 2D NMR spectra, it could be inferred that unit D (NRT linked to the O -3 of the adjacent unit) could also belong to this structure and could be attributed to the NRT of the main and/or side chain.

In addition, the DOSY NMR spectrum of YBGS showed that for this pool of polysaccharides, Unit F also presents the same diffusion time as unit A. For yeast β -glucans, the presence of unit F has been attributed to the side chains linked to a 3-linked β -D-glucose and/or to a 6-linkedβ -Dglucan branched at O -3 by one single glucose residue (Bastos et al., 2022; Lowman et al., 2011; Manners et al., 1973). The presence of unit H in NMR spectra of YBGS supports the presence of the latter. Although methylation analysis showed YBGS to contain 5 % of branching points, no correlations corresponding to these units could be identified, which could be attributed to the low content of branching points and overlapping of the signals. Therefore, it was not possible to infer if unit F is attached to unit A or it is another polysaccharide with similar M w.
