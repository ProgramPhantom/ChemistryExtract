Here it is possible to identify specified [ 1 H] environments that represent polymer size distribution (number average) based on the main monomer sidechain, the benzyl branching unit and multiple different chain end distributions. For low intensity (broad) 1 H signals (such as the benzl protons) there are clear shimming distortions that disrupt the data (see Fig. 2E) - but the fitting profiles of the Stejskal-Tanner plots shown in Fig. 2 indicate a reasonable fit to the produced decay curve despite inherent noise of the underlying data below the LCST.
