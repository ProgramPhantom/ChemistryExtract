The THF SEC system was used to provide Mp and the DOSY data provided the corresponding [ 𝛈 ]p for each PNIPAM standard in methanol. For calibration of the methanol system each sample was analyzed at a concentration of 1 mg ml -1 with a 100 𝛍 l injection loop. Fig. 3 shows that the data provided a linear Universal calibration plot, which was an indication that these PNIPAM standards were fractionated by size exclusion with minimal adsorption of the polymer to the column. Full data is shown in the electronic Supporting information.

To validate the technique a PNIPAM standard was purchased from Sigma Aldrich (quoted MV 63,200, Ð 2.13) and was analyzed by Methanol SEC.
