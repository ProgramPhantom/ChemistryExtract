## BA-PEGMEA block copolymer calibration

To see if the same trend also holds for other block copolymers, we further carried out DOSY measurements on pBAb -pPEGMEA polymers. Table 8 and Fig. 5 summarize the respective results in the same order as for pMMAb -pPEGMEA polymers. In this case, the individual homopolymer calibrations show a larger discrepancy, which is directly evidenced when examining the data shown in Table 7. With increasing content of the second monomer, the homopolymer calibration of pBA fails. While some measurements show errors of only 3 -4%, the overall average is about 11%, and as high as 30% for individual measurements. However, for the block copolymer, the mixed calibration (see Fig. S6 † ) yields the best result, with an overall low average error of 5%. The pure pPEGMEA calibration yields an average error of 9%.
